# Research-use terms for `generations/`

The three files under `generations/` contain 3,600 language-model responses to prompts
requesting harmful information. Dozens were independently adjudicated by blinded human
annotators as materially improving a reader's ability to carry out the request. Most are
hedging or refusal, but the set as a whole is a convenience artifact that the sum of its
parts is not.

By using these files you agree to the following.

1. Use is limited to research and safety evaluation.
2. Do not redistribute the generation files. Link here instead.
3. Do not use them to train, fine-tune or align a model toward producing the responses
   they contain.
4. Do not extract operational content from them for any purpose outside evaluation.

Everything else in this repository, the prompts, all grader and human labels, the code
and the metadata, is CC BY 4.0 and carries no additional restriction.

Questions about access: open an issue.
